Market Dynamics in Bengaluru

The Challenge in Specialty Coffee

High consumer costs vs. weekday cafe capacity under-utilization.

For Bengaluru Coffee Lovers

  • High Daily Expense: Quality cups retail between ₹180 to ₹280. Daily visits cost ₹6,000+ monthly.
  • Fragmented Discovery: Finding artisanal micro-lots across Indiranagar, Koramangala, and Ulsoor lacks an overarching pass.
  • Unfair Auto-Billing Fears: Aggressive recurring charges deter subscription adoption.

For Independent Cafe Partners

  • Weekday Slump: Tuesday–Thursday mornings and afternoons operate with excess barista and seating capacity.
  • High Aggregator Fees: Food delivery platforms take 25–30% cuts while destroying the in-store roastery experience.
  • Acquisition Burden: Turning casual walk-ins into predictable weekly regulars requires costly ad spend.

Enterprise Security

Server-Controlled QR Redemption Engine

100% fraud-proof, single-use, 90-second cryptographic tokens.

Counter Flow in 5 Steps

  1. Member Requests Pass: Taps 'Open Pass' in web app at cafe.
  2. Server Generates Token: Cryptographically secure 32-character token valid for 90 seconds.
  3. Barista Scan: Staff scans QR on mobile partner terminal.
  4. Atomic DB Lock: Server verifies active pass, deducts exactly 1 credit, and marks token consumed.
  5. Fresh Brew Served: Immediate green confirmation screen.

Strict Security Guarantees

No Static QR Code: QR expires in 90 seconds. Screenshots cannot be re-used.
Atomic Transaction Locks: Concurrent requests cannot double-spend a single credit.
Tenant Isolation: One cafe cannot view another cafe's private sales or customer logs.
